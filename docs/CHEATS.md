# Windows cheats

Open **Settings → Cheats**, choose your options and click **Apply**. Changes take
effect the next time you start the game. All cheats are off by default.

- **Infinite HP:** keeps living members of your player party at full health.
- **Infinite EP:** keeps their ability energy full and prevents normal ability costs.
- **Infinite money:** keeps TB at the game's limit of 9,999,999.
- **Infinite upgrade points:** keeps recruited characters' SP at 99,999, including reserves.
- **Money earned:** 1×, 2×, 5× or 10×. Multiplies positive TB gains; purchase costs stay normal.
  This selector is disabled while Infinite money is on.
- **Experience earned:** 1×, 2×, 5× or 10×. Multiplies positive experience awards for recruited characters.

**Turn all cheats off** restores normal options. Money, SP and earned levels
already recorded in a save remain there. Use a separate save slot if you want
to keep an unmodified playthrough.

HP and EP cheats pause during the opening Ice Queen fight, preserving its story
progression fix. They do not resurrect characters who were already defeated.
Enemy units are excluded. Rewards use the game's existing limits.

These options require Windows launcher 0.5.8 and native build 0.3.7. Existing
players must use **Update Game** with their supported ISO after updating the
launcher. The Linux preview is unchanged and has no Cheats tab.

Cheats have automated tests for party/enemy separation, the scripted opening
fight, invalid pointers, reward overflow, save-setting defaults and UI round trips.
Full-campaign cheat testing is still in progress.
