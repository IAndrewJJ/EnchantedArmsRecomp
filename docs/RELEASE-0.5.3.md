# 0.5.3 — Opening battle fix

Fixes the Game Over / Retry loop after **Ice Giga Zone** in the opening Queen
of Ice fight. Atsuma, Toya and Makoto now keep 1 HP after lethal attacks in that
fight, allowing the story to continue. Later fights keep their normal rules.

**Already playing?** Update the launcher, then choose **Update Game** and rebuild
from your ISO. Your saves and settings are kept. A launcher-only update cannot
apply this fix to an older compiled game.

The fix applies at 30 and 60 FPS. The opening story transition has been confirmed
by a player at 60 FPS, with the local game log verifying 1 HP for each character.
Full campaign testing is still in progress.

Download **EnchantedArmsPC-0.5.3-windows-x64.zip** from the release's Assets and
extract it before opening **EnchantedArmsLauncher.exe**. The package includes
the launcher and setup tools; you supply your own supported game ISO.
