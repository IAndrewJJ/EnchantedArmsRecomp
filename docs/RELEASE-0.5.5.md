# 0.5.5 — Choose your build folder

In **Settings → Game**, choose your ISO, then choose a **Build location**.
The game, saves and launcher will stay together in that folder. Use a dedicated
empty folder, such as **D:\Games\EnchantedArmsPC**.
The current build tools require a path without accented or non-Latin characters.

When setup finishes, the launcher reopens from your chosen folder. Click
**Play Enchanted Arms** when you're ready. Existing desktop shortcuts created
by the launcher are updated to point there.

If you already have a build, its current location stays selected. Choosing a
different folder copies your saves and texture packs; your original installation
is kept. Future launches from the original launcher open the completed build.

Updating from 0.5.4 does not require rebuilding the game. To build in another
location, choose that folder and click **Rebuild game**.

Download **EnchantedArmsPC-0.5.5-windows-x64.zip** from Assets and extract it
before opening **EnchantedArmsLauncher.exe**. Supply your own supported game ISO.
